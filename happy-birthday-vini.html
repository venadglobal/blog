Beyond your professional life, you carry hidden talents that fill our home with warmth. You're a devoted mother, and the boys absolutely cherish your recipes and the love you pour into your cooking. It's a joy watching you continuously evolve. The way you explore and master new technology is nothing short of exemplary. You balance the traditional warmth of a caregiver with the sharp, adaptable mind of a modern professional — not many people manage both at once.

Keep shining, keep brightening the lives of everyone around you, and keep building your beautiful legacy.

Happy Birthday, Vini!
