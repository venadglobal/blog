Kerala to Ireland —
The Complete Migration Guide 2026

CSEP €40,904 / GEP €36,605 from March 2026, Stamp 1G 24 months, Stamp 4 after 21 months, graduate discount €34k, NMBI nursing.

📅 Sept 2026 • Updated 19 Sep 2026 ✍️ Antony Ancil ⏱ 11 min read 🇮🇪 Ireland

Ireland is the only English-speaking country in the EU — and it's hiring aggressively. Dublin hosts European HQ of Google, Meta, Apple, Pfizer, Medtronic. For Kerala professionals, especially nurses and IT engineers, Ireland offers the smoothest language transition and a clear 2-year post-study work + 21-month path to Stamp 4 (PR equivalent).

This guide covers Sept 2026 — new salary thresholds from March 2026, Critical Skills vs General Employment Permits, Third Level Graduate Programme (Stamp 1G), and real costs.

🇮🇪 SEPT 2026 LIVE UPDATE — SALARY THRESHOLDS MARCH 2026

CSEP €40,904 / GEP €36,605 / HCA €32,691 from 1 March 2026

  • New thresholds 1 March 2026: DETE roadmap — CSEP €38k→€40,904 (degree) / €68,911 high-earner, GEP €34k→€36,605, HCA/Home Carer/Meat/Horticulture €30k→€32,691 — gradual rise till 2030
  • Graduate discount 2026: Recent graduates (Irish Level 9) get reduced thresholds €34,009-€36,848 — crucial for Stamp 1G → CSEP conversion
  • Processing 2026: 4-8 weeks standard, €1,000 fee — EPOS online system
  • Stamp 1G still 12/24 months: Level 8 Bachelor 12 months, Level 9 Master/PhD up to 24 months (12+12) — best post-study work in EU
  • Stamp 4 fast-track: CSEP holders eligible after 21 months — GEP after 60 months — Stamp 4 = no permit needed, live/work freely
💼 JOB — COMPREHENSIVE

Ireland job market Sept 2026 for Kerala: MedTech, pharma, IT, nursing, finance in extreme shortage. English-speaking = no language barrier for Kerala.

  • Kerala roles: Registered Nurses €35k-€55k, Software Engineers €50k-€80k, Medical Scientists, Pharmacists, Accountants, Chefs, Healthcare Assistants €32,691 threshold
  • Salary gate March 2026: CSEP €40,904 with degree relevant to job on Critical List — most IT/nursing jobs meet this — HCA route €32,691
  • Find jobs: IrishJobs.ie, Jobs.ie, LinkedIn Ireland, HSE jobs, Indeed.ie — filter “visa sponsorship / employment permit”
  • Gulf advantage: UAE healthcare (NMC-licensed) + Irish NMBI registration fast-track — GCC pharma/medtech MNC experience valued — 3+ years = CSEP eligible
🎓 STUDENTS — STUDY TO PR

Study pathway Sept 2026: Level 8/9 at ILEP-listed college → Stamp 2 (20h term / 40h holidays) → Stamp 1G 12/24 months (Third Level Graduate Programme) → CSEP €40,904 → Stamp 4 after 21 months → PR.

  • Top unis for Kerala: Trinity College Dublin, UCD, DCU, UCC, University of Galway — MSc Data Science, Pharma, Nursing, Business — fees €12k-€25k/year
  • Work rights: Stamp 2: 20h/week term-time, 40h holidays (June-Sept + Dec 15-Jan 15) — min wage €13.50/hr 2026
  • Post-study: Stamp 1G — 12 months Level 8, 24 months Level 9/10 — no permit needed during 1G, full labour market access — time to find CSEP job
  • Graduate discount: On 1G, you can get CSEP at €34,009-€36,848 if recent graduate (12 months from graduation) — key loophole for Kerala Masters students
  • Funds: €10,000 per year + tuition in account — show at immigration — plus private health insurance €150-300/year

The Big Picture — Two Main Pathways

Pathway 1 — Direct Employment Permit (CSEP / GEP) — Employer in Ireland applies for you via EPOS. CSEP is premium — faster Stamp 4 (21 months), immediate family reunification, spouse works. GEP is general — 60 months to Stamp 4, more paperwork.

Pathway 2 — Study → Stamp 1G → CSEP → Stamp 4 — Most Kerala students use this. Come as Masters student, get 2 years post-study to find €40,904 job, convert with graduate discount to €34k, then Stamp 4 after 21 months. Total 5 years to long-term residency.

Which Permit Is Right For You?

Degree + job on Critical Skills List (IT, nursing, engineering, accounting, medical scientist) + €40,904 offer — CSEP is no-brainer, fastest Stamp 4. Healthcare Assistant, horticulture, meat processing — GEP at €32,691 special threshold. No offer yet but Masters from Irish university — use Stamp 1G 24 months to job-hunt with graduate discount €34,009. Nurses — NMBI registration + CSEP, no labour market test.

Step-by-Step — How to Apply

1
Check Critical Skills List
DETE Critical Skills Occupations List — includes ICT, engineering, health, science, finance, construction. If your job is on list + you have relevant degree, you qualify for CSEP €40,904. If not on list but not on Ineligible List, GEP €36,605 route.
2
Secure Job Offer Meeting Threshold
Offer must state salary ≥ threshold, 39h week, 2 years, and include statement that you have relevant qualification. Employer must be registered with Revenue + CRO. For GEP, employer must do Labour Market Needs Test — advertise on EURES + IrishJobs for 28 days.
3
Employer Applies via EPOS
Employment Permits Online System — employer uploads contract, your degree, passport, job description. Fee €1,000. Processing 4-8 weeks 2026. You get permit letter — apply for D-type long-stay visa at VFS New Delhi/Bangalore if in India, or switch from Stamp 1G/Stamp 2 if in Ireland.
4
Enter Ireland & Register — IRP Card
At Dublin airport, show permit + visa — get entry stamp. Within 90 days, register at Burgh Quay Dublin or local Garda for IRP card (Irish Residence Permit). IRP + passport = your residence proof. Stamp 1 for permit holders.
5
Student Route — Stamp 2 to Stamp 1G
If student: study Level 8/9, maintain attendance, finish course — apply for Stamp 1G within 6 months of final results — bring final transcript + award letter + GNIB card. Stamp 1G gives 12 or 24 months full-time work without permit — use it to find CSEP job. Recent graduate discount applies.
6
21 Months → Stamp 4
After 21 months on CSEP, apply for Stamp 4 (Support Letter from DETE). Stamp 4 permission: work without permit, start business, no employer tie. This is de facto PR. Family on Stamp 4 can work without permit too.
7
Long-term & Citizenship
5 years on Stamp 4 → long-term residency. 5 years reckonable residence (last year continuous) → citizenship by naturalisation — need to be physically present 5 years out of 9, including 1 year continuous before application. Irish passport = EU + UK access.

For Kerala Nurses — The Irish Pathway

Ireland's HSE is actively recruiting Kerala nurses — but NMBI is strict.

Requirements 2026

  • NMBI Registration: BSc/GNM → NMBI Decision Letter — may require adaptation (6-12 weeks in Irish hospital) or aptitude test (RCSI). Fees €350 + adaptation €1,500-3,000 often employer-paid
  • Job Offer: HSE or private hospital offers €35k-€45k — meets CSEP €40,904 threshold — no labour market test — immediate family reunification
  • English: IELTS 7.0 in all bands / OET B for NMBI — higher than work permit English — prepare first
  • HCA Route: If not NMBI-ready, Healthcare Assistant permit €32,691 — work while completing NMBI — but Stamp 4 after 5 years, not 21 months
  • Cost: NMBI €350, visa €60, flight, initial accommodation €1,500 — total Rs.3-4 Lakh, often employer refunds

The Honest Reality

  • Thresholds rising till 2030 — March 2026 is first increase — future years will be higher — apply sooner rather than later if salary near threshold.
  • Accommodation crisis is real — Dublin rent €1,800-€2,500 for 1-bed — budget €1,200 shared — employers often give 1 month initial stay for nurses.
  • Only ILEP colleges give Stamp 1G — many private colleges lost ILEP status 2024-25 — check official ILEP list before paying fees — no ILEP = no 1G.
  • CSEP is vastly better than GEP — 21 months vs 60 months to Stamp 4 + immediate family — aim for Critical List job even if salary slightly lower but meets €40,904.
  • Graduate discount is key — Masters students: your CSEP threshold drops to €34,009-€36,848 if you apply within 12 months of graduation — don't waste time after course.

Final Word

Ireland in Sept 2026 remains the easiest English-speaking EU move for Kerala — especially nurses and IT. Salary thresholds just increased March 2026 to €40,904 (CSEP) / €36,605 (GEP) / €32,691 (HCA) — but still achievable, and graduate discount lowers it further to €34,009.

Direct work route: 4-8 months to landing if you have NMBI or Critical List job. Student route: 1-year Masters €12k-€25k → 24 months Stamp 1G → CSEP → Stamp 4 in 21 months. No German/Spanish needed, English only, and Irish passport gives you UK + EU access. Budget Rs.4-6 Lakh for work route, Rs.15-25 Lakh for study route including first year.
