HAPPY 4th BIRTHDAY
NILA & NEIL!

Double the blessing, double the grace — the best is yet to come! 💕

There are moments in life that redefine our understanding of joy, and exactly four years ago today, our world was doubly blessed.

On July 29th, 2022, after thirteen years of unwavering faith, love, and patience, two brilliant professionals — Jesny John (Professor at All Saints' College, Trivandrum) and Nimesh Das (Mathrubhumi TV) — saw their prayers answered not just once, but twice. They welcomed two beautiful souls into the world: Nila Maria Grace and Neil Alden Das.

Today, as they turn four, we celebrate the light, the laughter, and the boundless energy they bring to everyone fortunate enough to be in their orbit.

🌳 The Family Roots

Das Papa's Legacy

A family's architecture is only as strong as its foundation. Today, we hold a special place in our hearts for Nimesh's late father, Professor Das of Fatima Matha National College. "Das Papa" was everything to this family. Though he physically departed before he could hold Nila and Neil, his wisdom, warmth, and guiding spirit are forever woven into their DNA. He is watching over them with immense pride.

Grandmother Grace's Dedication

Beside this towering legacy stands their beloved grandmother, Grace Das, who is the absolute bedrock of the family today. Her excellence and unwavering dedication to these children provide the beautiful, loving environment in which these twins are thriving.

🕊️ A Dual Covenant of Guidance

For Nila

Vini and I consider it one of our life's greatest honors to stand as your godparents. Our promise is to always be a steadfast pillar of support and a safe harbor for you as you navigate life.

For Neil

Guided by the loving hands of his godparents, Jithesh Das and Theresa, Neil has a profound source of strength, wisdom, and family heritage to lean on as he grows into a young man.

Together, as godparents, we stand beside Jesny and Nimesh to help guide, protect, and celebrate these two bright stars.

🎈 To the Twins on Their 4th Birthday
👧

Nila Maria Grace

May your life always reflect the grace of your namesake. Keep shining with that beautiful curiosity, your sweet spirit, and your radiant smile.

👦

Neil Alden Das

May you grow with the strength, intellect, and protective nature of a true leader. Keep exploring, keep laughing, and keep being the wonderful brother that you are.

🦅 To Jesny and Nimesh

Balancing highly demanding careers in academia and media while raising twins is the ultimate masterclass in patience, love, and partnership. Watching the two of you build this beautiful family after a thirteen-year wait is a testament to your resilience. You are phenomenal parents, and these two angels are so lucky to have you steering their ship.

Happy Birthday, Little Ones! 🎂✨

May your 4th birthday be filled with endless laughter, too much cake, and the overwhelming love of your incredible family. The best is yet to come.

With all our love and blessings,
From Akil, Adil, Vini & Antony, Evan, Juan, Seena, Jeneesh, Valsala and John
