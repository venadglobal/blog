KSERC MYT Regulations 2026: The Rs 5,000 Crore Regulatory Asset & FPPAS Trap From April 2027

Final notification dated 17 Oct 2026 — Past losses of Rs 5000 crore to be recovered from you for 4 years, fuel surcharge now includes costly short-term power at Rs 12, and peak extended to 11:30 PM. KSEB drafts, KSERC — staffed by ex-KSEB — asks KSEB for clarifications.

19 OCT 2026 ANTONY ANCIL CONTROL PERIOD 2027-28 TO 2031-32

On 17th October 2026 (Saturday), Kerala State Electricity Regulatory Commission published its final Multi-Year Tariff Regulations, 2026 for five-year control period from 1st April 2027 onwards. Existing 2021 regulations expire March 2027. This is not about connection charges like OP 34/2026 — this decides HOW your monthly bill will be calculated for next 5 years. And inside it, two traps that make OP 34/2026 slab-trap look small.

01. What Changed

KSEB filed tariff framework petition based on regulatory gap accumulated up to 31st March 2024. Public hearings held 29-30 Sep 2026 after draft published 24 Aug 2026. Addendum on 8 Sep 2026. Final order published 17 Oct 2026.

As per The Hindu 19 Oct 2026: "The measures are part of the Kerala State Electricity Regulatory Commission's multi-year tariff regulations for the five-year control period from 2027-28 onwards. They will take effect on April 1, 2027. The existing thermal fuel surcharge formula has been replaced with a Fuel and Power Purchase Cost Adjustment Surcharge (FPPAS) mechanism."

At a glance:
• Control Period: 1 Apr 2027 – 31 Mar 2032 (5 years)
• RA Surcharge Period: 2027-28, 28-29, 29-30, 30-31 (4 years)
• Old Formula: Only long-term CGS & IPP purchases counted for fuel surcharge
• New FPPAS: Total energy from ALL sources — long, medium, short-term — counted
• Peak Redefined: 8am-6pm solar, 6pm-11:30pm peak, 11:30pm-8am off-peak
• New Entity: Energy Storage System Developers (ESSD) recognised — 85% BESS efficiency, 75% PSP
• Legal Basis: Compliance with Centre's Electricity (Amendment) Rules, 2022

03. TRAP 1 — Rs 5,000 Crore Regulatory Asset Surcharge

This is the biggest recovery — first time in Kerala history RA surcharge is being collected from KSEB consumers.

As per The Hindu 19 Oct 2026: "A new regulatory asset surcharge (RA surcharge) will be levied on electricity bills over a four-year period as part of a decision to liquidate the regulatory assets of distribution licencees including that of the KSEB by March 31. The regulatory asset of the KSEB for liquidation would amount to roughly Rs 5,000 crore."

As per Mathrubhumi 19 Oct 2026: "Kerala consumers are likely to face higher electricity bills from April 1, 2027, as the State Electricity Regulatory Commission has notified a tariff policy to recover the Kerala State Electricity Board's past losses of Rs 5,000 crore over four years through surcharges."

How it will be billed — Two formulas

For demand-based consumers (industrial, commercial with demand meter): RA surcharge billed at a percentage of tariff invoice (sum of demand charge + energy charge).

For all other consumers (LT domestic, LT commercial): Combination of 'rate per kW per month' + percentage of energy charges. It will also be first instance of RA surcharge being collected.

Practical impact: If your bill is Rs 2,000/month and RA surcharge is 10% (estimate), you pay Rs 200 extra x 48 months = Rs 9,600 for electricity consumed in 2021-2024. You pay for yesterday's electricity at tomorrow's rate. And as Times of India noted — recovery would be in four equal instalments after commission considers accounts for 2023-24. So amount may even increase after true-up.

04. TRAP 2 — FPPAS: Costly Short-Term Power in Your Bill

Old System (till 31 Mar 2027): At present, KSEB's purchases under long-term agreements from central generating stations (CGS) and independent power producers (IPP) alone are computed to derive monthly surcharge.

New FPPAS System (from 1 Apr 2027): Under FPPAS, purchases from ALL sources, including long-term, medium-term and short-term power purchases, will be considered, meaning that amount to be recovered under monthly automatic recovery mechanism would be higher.

Why this is dangerous: Kerala is battling hard to stave off power shortages (Hindu 19 Oct). In summer 2026, KSEB bought short-term power at Rs 8-12/unit from exchanges. Earlier that cost was not fully passed monthly — it went through truing-up after year. Now it will be passed directly next month via FPPAS. Your bill will spike in summer not because you used more, but because KSEB bought costly power.

05. TRAP 3 — Peak Hours Extended 6PM-10PM to 6PM-11:30PM

Mathrubhumi Explainer 19 Oct 2026: "The peak-hour period, previously set from 6 pm to 10 pm, will now extend from 6 pm to 11.30 pm. The longer period could increase the duration for which consumers are charged higher rates."

Who suffers most: Domestic families who cook, study, watch TV 7-11pm. Small shops open till 10:30pm. All now in extended peak. Times of India draft says day divided into three zones: 8am-6pm solar, 6pm-11:30pm peak, 11:30pm-8am off-peak. Tariffs lower solar/off-peak, higher evening peak. But 80% domestic consumption is evening.

06. TRAP 4 — 4% Auto Pass-Through Without Prior Approval

Central Electricity (Amendment) Rules, 2022 allow Discoms to automatically pass through variations in fuel and power purchase cost, subject to true-up by commission.

Rule: KSEB can recover additional cost directly from consumers without prior approval. Direct recovery limited to 4% of average electricity price when collected without commission approval. Licensee may defer adjustment for up to two billing cycles.

Math: 4% per month = if applied 12 months, 48% potential annual variation via monthly surcharges. Deferral for 2 months means sudden 8% jump in 3rd month possible. Subject to annual true-up — but refund after 1 year, if at all.

07. TRAP 5 — ToD Meters Compulsory

Policy calls for time-of-day (ToD) meters to be installed for more consumer categories to enable differential billing based on time electricity is used. Lower tariffs during off-peak hours, from 8am-6pm and 11:30pm-8am.

Means smart meter installation for LT consumers. Cost, timeline, who pays — not yet disclosed. But once ToD meter in, same 1 unit at 2pm vs 8pm will have different rate. Your bill will depend on WHEN you use, not just HOW MUCH.

08. What Is Still Protected

Protection 1 — FPPAS Exemption: FPPAS will apply to all consumers, except domestic consumers with connected load of and below 1,000 Watts and those having a monthly consumption of and below 40 units. — The Hindu 19 Oct 2026

Protection 2 — No hike for 2026-27: As per KSERC tariff orders, no increase in tariff approved for 2026-27. Real hike starts 1 Apr 2027 — you have 5 months to prepare.

Protection 3 — Storage Incentive: Consumers who install electricity storage at home and supply power to grid at night should receive incentivising rates. Plus green tariff option proposed.

Protection 4 — True-up & Safeguards: FPPAS subject to annual truing-up. Licensee may defer for 2 billing cycles, higher adjustments subject to additional scrutiny. Use this to demand refund.

10. RTI Toolkit — 4 Questions to Break the Closed Loop

As we did for OP 34/2026 slab-trap, use RTI Rs 10 x 4 = Rs 40 to break KSEB-KSERC closed loop. KSEB drafts proposal, sends 500 pages annexure that only KSEB can interpret, KSERC staffed by ex-KSEB asks KSEB for clarifications, KSEB clarifies itself.

Question 1 — Regulatory Asset True-Up — Rs 5000cr Ka Hisaab

Request certified copy of regulatory asset amount finally approved after considering accounts for 2023-24 (as per TOI draft), breakup of principal vs interest, category-wise surcharge % approved, and copy of KSEB petition claiming Rs 5000cr. If Rs 5000cr includes interest on interest, it is compound recovery.

Question 2 — FPPAS Monthly Sheet — Short-Term Ka Sach

Request month-wise power purchase cost for FY 2025-26 and 2026-27 — long-term CGS rate, medium-term rate, short-term exchange rate separately, total units, total cost. This will expose how much Rs 10-12/unit short-term power is being loaded into FPPAS. Also ask copy of formula for FPPAS calculation as per final 17 Oct notification.

Question 3 — Peak Extension Data — 6PM-11:30PM Justification

Request Kerala grid load curve data for last 2 years (2024-25, 2025-26) — hourly demand from 6pm-11:30pm, solar generation curve 8am-6pm, and note file approving extension from 4 hrs to 5.5 hrs. If demand 10pm-11:30pm is lower than 6pm-10pm, why extension? Ask for copy of study relied upon.

Question 4 — ToD Meter Rollout — Kaun Bharega?

Request copy of ToD meter specification, cost per meter, who pays — consumer or KSEB, timeline for LT domestic, commercial categories, whether mandatory, and copy of cost-benefit analysis showing lower daytime rates benefit vs higher evening rates loss for average domestic consumer (200 units/month).

11. Checklist Before 1st April 2027

  • Check exemption: If connected load ≤1000W and consumption ≤40 units — you are exempt from FPPAS. Keep load certificate ready.
  • Shift heavy loads to 8am-6pm: Washing machine, water heater, pump, EV charging — move to solar hours if ToD applies. Save 1.5 hrs peak premium.
  • Track 2 new lines from April bill: Bill will have FPPAS + RA surcharge separately — note % each month, preserve bills. This is your evidence for true-up refund.
  • Measure peak usage now: Check how many units you use 6PM-11:30PM for 1 week — that is future costliest slot. Plan reduction.
  • File objection if RA beyond 2030-31: Order says RA surcharge 2027-28 to 2030-31 only. If it appears after 31 Mar 2031, cite final notification 17 Oct 2026 and file complaint to KSERC + CGRF.
  • Demand online calculator: Like OP 34/2026 order Para 7 directed online per-kW calculator, demand online FPPAS calculator from KSEB — formula should be public.
Kerala does not have a tariff problem. It has a past-cost recovery problem.
KSEB does not have a revenue gap. It has an accountability gap — Rs 5,000 crore gap that was not trued-up for years, now put on your monthly bill.
We do not have independent regulation. We have automatic pass-through — 4% per month, without prior approval.

MYT 2026 recognises storage, green tariffs — welcome. But Rs 5,000 crore RA surcharge for 4 years, FPPAS including Rs 12 short-term power, and peak extended to 11:30 PM means you pay for yesterday's short-term purchases at tomorrow's peak rate. Cross-check the order, not just the bill. That is how citizens bell the cat.
