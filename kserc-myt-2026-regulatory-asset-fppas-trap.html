01. What Changed

KSEB filed tariff framework petition based on regulatory gap accumulated up to 31st March 2024. Public hearings held 29-30 Sep 2026 after draft published 24 Aug 2026. Addendum on 8 Sep 2026. Final order published 17 Oct 2026 by State regulator.

As per The Hindu 19 Oct 2026: "The measures are part of the Kerala State Electricity Regulatory Commission's multi-year tariff regulations for the five-year control period from 2027-28 onwards. They will take effect on April 1, 2027. The existing thermal fuel surcharge formula has been replaced with a Fuel and Power Purchase Cost Adjustment Surcharge (FPPAS) mechanism."

At a glance — State Government Framework:
• Control Period: 1 Apr 2027 – 31 Mar 2032 (5 years) — notified by State regulator KSERC
• RA Surcharge Period: 2027-28 to 2030-31 — Rs 5,000 crore recovery approved by State regulator for State PSU
• Legal Basis Cited: State regulator cites Central Electricity (Amendment) Rules, 2022 as enabling provision, but decision is by State regulator, not Central Government
• Peak Redefined by State: 8am-6pm solar, 6pm-11:30pm peak, 11:30pm-8am off-peak — State decision, not Central
• Owner: KSEB Ltd is 100% owned by State Government — not Central PSU

08. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE? State Government
Not Central, Not KSEB Alone

This is the central question this blog answers. Public blames KSEB, analysis questions KSERC, but institutional truth is State Government is 100% responsible. Not Central Government. Central Government only made enabling Rules in 2022. State Government is the one that uses those Rules to recover money, appoints regulator, owns utility, and hides information.

Three Proofs That State Government Is Behind This Mess:

PROOF 1 — State Government Appoints KSERC (Not Central): Under Section 82 of Electricity Act, 2003, KSERC Chairman and Members are appointed by State Government. Many members are ex-KSEB, ex-Power Department, ex-Chief Secretary level IAS. This creates closed loop: State Government drafts proposal via KSEB, sends 500-page annexure only KSEB can interpret, State Government appointed regulator asks KSEB for clarification, KSEB clarifies itself. Consumer never gets independent scrutiny.

PROOF 2 — State Government Owns KSEB Ltd (Not Central PSU): KSEB Ltd is 100% owned by Government of Kerala. Board, CMD, Commercial wing that prepared B.O. 358/2026 for RMD charges, tariff petition for Rs 5,000 crore — all approved by State Government before reaching KSERC. KSEB is not private company running business — it is State PSU running on State Government decisions. Salary, pension 46.59% of cost is State Government approved expense.

PROOF 3 — State Government Delays Created This Gap: Regulatory asset of Rs 5,000 crore did not fall from sky. It is accumulation of State Government's policy failure: did not revise tariff on time 2021-2024 for vote bank, did not pay subsidy on time, did not collect Rs 3,000 crore arrears from Government departments (LSGD, PWD, Water Authority), did not reduce staff cost, did not fix theft and commercial loss. When gap became too big, easy solution: put on honest paying domestic consumer via RA surcharge + FPPAS + extended peak. State Government does not know how to do business and make money for State — so it taxes via electricity bill.

State Government vs Central Government — Clear Distinction:

Conclusion: Central Government gave knife, but State Government is the one cutting consumer's pocket. Blame lies with State Government.
