✦ A Poem — by idivet

What Simba Knows

On unconditional presence. On being seen without performance. On a warm weight beside you when the screen goes dark.

🐾 Simba ✍ Antony Ancil 📍 Kollam, Kerala May 2026
← Back to all posts

He doesn't ask where you've been

or why you came back quieter than you left.

He doesn't count the years

or measure what was lost

against what remains.

When the rain speaks to the roof

and the world outside argues with itself —

he finds you.

Not the version you show others.

The one that sits alone

after the screen goes dark.

He has seen you celebrated

and he has seen you uncertain.

He loved the same man both times.

No certificate hangs on his wall.

No approval pending.

No grievance filed.

Just this —

a warm weight beside you,

breathing slowly,

reminding you

that presence

is its own kind of answer.

Thirty years the world asked

what do you do?

what have you built?

what do you have?

Simba only ever asked —

are you here?

And you always were.

That was always enough.

Simba — the inspiration
Full black pug. Kollam, Kerala. Doesn't ask questions. Just shows up. Every single time.
