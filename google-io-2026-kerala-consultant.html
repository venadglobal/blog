I have been watching technology change the world for three decades. From the first email systems at HCT Abu Dhabi in 1999 to building distance learning classrooms in 2008 before anyone knew what remote learning was — I have always believed that the people who adapt survive, and the people who wait get left behind.

Google I/O 2026 was different from previous years. This was not about new phones or updated apps. This was about AI becoming the operating layer of everything. And for someone like me — a consultant reskilling in Kollam, Kerala, after 30 years in UAE — the announcements were not abstract. They were immediately actionable.

✦ 3 Takeaways
Takeaway 01 — Search
Gemini 3.5 Flash is now in Search — 4x faster, smarter, deeper

Google announced that Gemini 3.5 Flash is now integrated directly into Google Search. The result? Search is no longer just a list of links. It reasons, synthesises and answers. For a consultant doing market research for a client, this is transformational.

I am currently completing Google AI Essentials on Coursera (Financial Aid approved). Everything I am learning about prompting and working with Gemini just got a direct real-world application. The AI I study in the morning, I can use to research a client's market by the afternoon.

How I am applying this: Using Gemini in Search to research destination country job markets for VGC clients — Canada, Australia, UK, Germany — in minutes instead of hours. What used to take a full day of browser tabs now takes one well-crafted prompt.
Takeaway 02 — Multimodal Search
The new Search box accepts images and video — not just text

Google showed that you can now drop a photo or video into the Search bar and ask questions about it. This is not a gimmick. For small business consultants and immigration advisors, this changes how we work with clients.

A client in Kollam can send me a photo of a document — a qualification certificate, a property notice, a visa form — and I can instantly research it using AI without having to type out every detail. The friction between receiving information and acting on it is disappearing.

How I am applying this: When clients send photos of foreign qualification certificates or job offer letters, I can now drop them directly into Search and get instant context — country-specific regulations, recognition pathways, salary benchmarks. No more manual lookup.
Takeaway 03 — Agentic AI
AI is moving from prompts to action — the skill is now thinking, not typing

The biggest announcement at Google I/O 2026 was the shift to agentic workflows — AI that doesn't just answer questions but takes sequences of actions to complete a task. Google's Project Mariner. NotebookLM going deeper. Gemini acting as an agent across apps.

This means the competitive advantage is no longer who can type the best prompt. The advantage now belongs to the person who can think clearly about what they want and break it into logical steps for AI to execute. That is a consultancy skill. That is a human skill.

How I am applying this: Structuring VGC's client workflows — intake, research, document prep, application — as sequences that AI can assist with at every step. The consultant's job is no longer doing every step. It is knowing which steps matter and in what order.

Why this matters if you are in Kerala

Kerala has one of the most internationally mobile populations in the world. Over 2 million Keralites work in the Gulf alone. Hundreds of thousands more are in the UK, Canada, Australia and beyond. Every single one of them — and every family member supporting their journey — is affected by the speed and quality of information they can access.

When I returned to Kollam after 30 years in UAE, I saw consultants still working the way they did in 2005. Paper files. Phone calls for information that takes 30 seconds to find online. That gap is an opportunity.

Google I/O 2026 confirmed what I already believed: the consultants who survive the next five years will be the ones who build AI into every step of their workflow. Not as a replacement for human judgment — but as a force multiplier for it.
