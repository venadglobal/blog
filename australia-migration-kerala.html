Kerala to Australia —
The Complete Migration Guide 2026

Points system, visa subclasses, AHPRA nursing registration, real costs and timeline. Everything Kerala professionals need to know about migrating to Australia in 2026.

📅 May 2026 ✍️ Antony Ancil ⏱ 10 min read 🦘 Australia

Australia is home to one of the largest Kerala communities outside India. From nurses in Melbourne hospitals to IT engineers in Sydney, from teachers in Queensland to construction workers in Perth — Malayalees have built deep roots across the continent.

And Australia is actively looking for more. The country runs one of the world's most structured skilled migration programmes — transparent, points-based, and genuinely merit-driven. If your skills are in demand and your points are competitive, Australia is genuinely achievable.

This guide covers the full picture for 2026 — from the points system to nursing registration, from costs to realistic timelines. No sugarcoating. Just what you need to know.

The Big Picture — Two Main Pathways

Pathway 1 — General Skilled Migration (GSM) — the points-based system. No employer needed. You score points based on age, education, work experience, and English — and compete in a pool for invitations.

Pathway 2 — Employer Sponsored — an Australian employer sponsors you directly. Bypasses the competitive points pool but requires finding a willing employer.

The Three Main Skilled Visa Subclasses

189
Skilled Independent
No state or employer needed. Live anywhere. Permanent residency from day one. Most competitive — needs highest points.
190
Skilled Nominated
State nominates you. +5 bonus points. Permanent residency. Live in nominating state 2 years. More accessible than 189.
491
Skilled Regional
5-year provisional visa. Regional areas. +15 points for nomination. Pathway to PR after 3 years. Most accessible entry point.
Which Visa Is Right For You?

High points (80+) and want freedom — go for 189. Moderate points (65-80) — look at 190 with state nomination. Lower points — 491 is the strategic entry point. Many migrants use 491 first, build Australian experience, and transition to permanent residency via 191.

The Points System — What You Need to Know

The minimum score to enter the pool is 65 points. But the honest reality — 65 is rarely enough. For most occupations in 2026, you need 80-90+ points to be competitive for Subclass 189.

Step-by-Step — How to Apply

1
Check Your Occupation on the Skilled Occupation List
Your occupation must be on Australia's Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL) for Subclass 189, or the Short-term Skilled Occupation List (STSOL) for 190/491. Healthcare, IT, engineering, education, and trades are well represented. Check: immi.homeaffairs.gov.au
2
Get a Skills Assessment
Engineers Australia for engineers. ACS for IT professionals. ANMAC for nurses. VETASSESS for general professions. Timeline: 4-12 weeks. Cost: AUD 500-1,000 depending on authority.
3
Sit an English Test
IELTS Academic, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, or OET. Aim for IELTS 8+ in all bands for maximum points (20 points). At minimum you need IELTS 6 in all bands. The difference between 7.0 and 8.0 is worth 10 extra points — significant in a competitive pool.
4
Submit Expression of Interest via SkillSelect
Create a profile on SkillSelect at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au. Submit your EOI with calculated points. You enter a pool and wait for invitation rounds. Frequency depends on occupation and visa subclass.
5
Receive Invitation to Apply
When invited, you have 60 days to lodge your full visa application. This is when you pay the visa application fee of AUD 4,640.
6
Lodge Full Visa Application
Submit all documents — skills assessment, English test, police clearances, health checks, qualification certificates, employment references. Processing time: 6-12 months.
7
Visa Granted — Welcome to Australia
Subclass 189 and 190 — permanent residency from day one. Subclass 491 — 5-year provisional visa with pathway to PR after 3 years regional living.

For Kerala Nurses — The AHPRA Pathway

Kerala nurses must register with AHPRA (Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency) before they can practise in Australia. This runs parallel to the visa application.

AHPRA Requirements 2026

  • Qualification: BSc Nursing — assessed by ANMAC
  • English: IELTS 7.0 in ALL four bands individually (not just overall average) — OR OET Grade B in all sections. No exceptions.
  • Good Standing Certificate from Kerala Nursing Council
  • International Criminal History Check
⚠️ Critical — IELTS for AHPRA

AHPRA requires 7.0 in EVERY individual band — not overall 7.0. Many nurses fail because they score 6.5 in Writing even when the overall average is 7.0. Prepare specifically for Writing. OET's clinical writing tasks are often more manageable for nurses.

The Honest Reality

  • 65 points is not enough for most occupations — 80-90+ is the competitive reality for Subclass 189
  • Regional Australia is genuinely viable — Adelaide, Perth, Canberra count as "regional" for 491
  • Gulf work experience counts — every year in UAE, Saudi, Qatar adds to your points
  • Partner skills add points — if your spouse has a skilled occupation, you gain extra points
  • Age 45+ is not disqualifying — but you need strong points elsewhere

Final Word

Australia is genuinely one of the best migration destinations for Kerala professionals. The points system is transparent and merit-based. Healthcare actively recruits Indian nurses. IT welcomes skilled engineers. The Malayalee community means you will never feel alone.

The process takes 2-3 years from start to landing. Budget Rs.5-7 Lakh minimum. But permanent residency in one of the world's most liveable countries, with AUD salaries that make the investment worthwhile, is within realistic reach.

Start with your points calculation. Be honest about your score. Choose the visa subclass that gives you the best realistic shot.
