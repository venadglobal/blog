Happy Birthday, Shajy Leo Koshy:
A Tribute to a Life
Exceptionally Lived

A true elder brother, in every sense that matters.

✍️ Antony Ancil — Kollam, Kerala 📅 August 5

Wishing a very Happy Birthday to a man who transcends the title of brother-in-law and stands as a true elder brother to Bro John and me.

There are very few people who can navigate the complexities of life with such grace, resilience, and quiet strength. Today, we celebrate not just your birthday, but the remarkable "marathon life" you've built, the legacy you continue to shape, and the deep respect you command from everyone fortunate enough to know you.

🦅 A Marathon of Success and Resilience

Your journey is nothing short of extraordinary. From your discipline as a martial arts expert to your commanding role as GM of SIS International for offshore activities in Abu Dhabi, you've always led from the front. You mastered the art of business, bringing visions to life like the Golden Valley Resort at Kallar, Ponmudi.

When you made the bold decision to leave Abu Dhabi in 2000, you didn't step back — you elevated. Today, reigning peacefully from the top floor of the Leo Group Tower, you are the architect of your own well-earned empire.

🤝 The Gentleman and the Quiet Guardian

You possess a rare, outgoing personality that instantly puts people at ease. Your hospitality is remarkable — every visit to your home is met with warmth and genuine care. You're the kind of man who always finds time to appreciate the achievements of others.

You have a profound ability to accept everyone and engage smoothly in any conversation, while remaining firmly rooted in your own ideologies. You're strict on your life protocols and never take anything for granted.

Behind that strong, resilient exterior is a heart that worries deeply for everyone — secretly checking in, always asking about our mother's health, and guarding the family without ever letting anyone see the weight you carry.

🐾 The Adventurer and Animal Lover

Age restrictions may try to slow down the water sports and hiking, but your love for nature safaris and the great outdoors remains untouched. That fierce compassion extends to your incredible passion for animals — raising majestic breeds like Rottweilers, American Bullies, and the Tibetan Mastiff with genuine love and care.

🏛️ The Patriarch and His Proud Legacy

Your greatest pride, alongside Seema, is undoubtedly the family you've built. Watching Adhithya and Greeshma, along with Aariyan, thriving and making their mark at Technopark is a testament to the foundation you provided. Surrounded by the love of your family, your respected circle of friends at the Trivandrum Club, and your esteemed sister Dr. Sheila in Ernakulam, your life is rich with meaningful connections.

🌅 The Balcony Philosopher

Perhaps the most iconic image of you is spending your evenings on the balcony of the Leo Tower, facing Shankumugham beach with the ambiance of a 5-star resort, taking in the world with a uniquely sharp eye. Whether watching naval exercises or noticing the subtle beach activities the rest of us miss, you observe life from a higher perspective.

"May you continue to enjoy the view from the top of the tower —
and may the year ahead bring immense peace, joy, and excellent health."

Happy Birthday, Shajy!

Many, many happy returns of the day.

All the best wishes from Adhithya, Aaryan, Seema, Adil, Akil, Vini, Antony, John and Violet.
