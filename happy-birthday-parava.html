Sister • Teacher • Warrior • My Parava

Happy Birthday, Parava Prabha Ann Mary

For the woman who turned every struggle into a lesson, every loss into light — brilliant teacher, beautiful soul, and the strongest sister I know.

12 September 2026Venad Global BlogBy Antony Ancil
✦ Click photo to unlock Resilience Protocol ✦

Some people enter our lives as colleagues. Some as friends. And then there are rare ones — who become sisters. Parava, you are that rare one.

Vini's friend, colleague. My sister-like. If someone asks me what strength looks like in real life — I don't think of a quote. I think of you, Prabha Ann Mary.

01

The Brilliant Teacher

You don't just teach English. You make it breathe. In your classroom, Shakespeare isn't distant, grammar isn't scary, and every child believes they can write, speak, and dream in a language that once felt foreign.

I've watched students light up when you explain a poem. That is not just teaching — that's magic. And that magic comes from years of dedication, from loving a subject so deeply that it becomes a gift you give away every single day.

What they say in the staffroom

“If you want the English to be brilliant — ask Prabha Miss. If you want the class to be loved — ask Parava.” That's the reputation you built, not with noise, but with quiet, consistent excellence.

02

The Strength Behind the Smile

Life has not been gentle with you, Parava. You've walked through struggles that would make most people give up. You've faced loss that no words can hold — and recently, the deepest one.

And yet — look at you. Still standing in that beautiful saree, holding flowers, smiling with such grace that the camera itself bows.

“She didn't survive because she had it easy. She survived because she decided that falling was not her final chapter.”

People see the beautiful, well-dressed teacher. I see the warrior who wakes up every morning and chooses courage. Who carries grief with dignity. Who turns pain into wisdom for her students. That is true boldness — not loud, but unbreakable.

Beautiful

Not just in a saree under chandeliers, but in how you carry yourself — with elegance even on hard days.

Bold

You speak truth, stand tall, and never shrink yourself to make others comfortable.

Brilliant

English is your subject, but clarity is your nature. You explain life the way you explain literature — with depth.

Brave

You've rebuilt yourself more than once, and each time you came back softer, stronger, wiser.

03

Still Becoming, Still Shining

This birthday is not just a celebration of age. It's a celebration of resilience. Of a woman who has been a daughter, a wife, a mother, a teacher, a friend, a sister — and excelled at being human through it all.

You taught us that strength is not about never breaking. It's about holding yourself together with grace after you break, and then helping others stitch their own stories too.

Happy Birthday, My Dear Parava

May this year be gentle with you. May your classroom be filled with more laughter.
May your heart find peace in small things — morning tea, good books, children's essays, evening flowers.

You deserve love that stays, joy that is quiet and deep, and days where you don't have to be strong — you can just be.

From your brother — Antony Ancil • Vini's family • Venad Global • With respect and affection always

We don't call you Parava just as a nickname. In Malayalam, Parava means a dove — but you are not just a soft bird. You are a dove that flew through storms and still knows how to find home.

Keep shining, keep teaching, keep being you.
We are proud to call you ours.

Happy Birthday, Prabha Ann Mary.
